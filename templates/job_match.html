How the Skills Matcher Works

Enter your technical skills above. The engine scans the requirements JSON column for every role in MySQL and calculates an exact match score:

Match Percentage = (Matched Skills Count / Total Required Skills) × 100%

Jobs are ranked from highest to lowest compatibility so you can focus on roles where your resume stands out.
